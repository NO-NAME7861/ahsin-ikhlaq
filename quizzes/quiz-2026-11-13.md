## Quiz 1

**If you fell into a black hole feet-first, what would spaghettification feel like first? 🍝🕳️**

A) Instant vaporization at the event horizon
B) A gentle stretch, then snap at the singularity
C) Your feet pulled harder than your head, stretching you like taffy
D) Time stops so you feel nothing at all

Answer: C
Explanation: Tidal forces stretch you vertically while compressing horizontally — classic spaghettification.

## Quiz 2

**The Stoics watched stars to practice 'cosmic perspective.' What does that actually mean? ✨🏛️**

A) Predicting eclipses to impress friends
B) Memorizing constellations for navigation
C) Seeing your worries as specks against 13.8 billion years
D) Worshipping planets as gods

Explanation: Cosmic perspective = realizing your problems are tiny in deep time and infinite space.
