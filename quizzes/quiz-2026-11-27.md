## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=14.2 — light left when the universe was only ~280 Myr old. How can we see it if the universe wasn't transparent yet? ✨**

A) Gravitational lensing by a foreground cluster
B) Reionization bubbles let UV photons escape early
C) The galaxy's black hole outshines the fog
D) JWST sees infrared, which penetrates neutral hydrogen

Answer: B
Explanation: Early galaxies ionized local bubbles, letting Lyman-alpha photons leak out before full reionization.

## Quiz 2

**Seneca wrote: 'We suffer more in imagination than in reality.' Modern neuroscience calls this the brain's ______ system overpredicting threat. 🧠**

A) dopaminergic reward
B) default mode network
C) amygdala-prefrontal
D) predictive processing

Answer: D
Explanation: Predictive processing models show the brain constantly simulates worst cases — exactly what Stoics warned about.
