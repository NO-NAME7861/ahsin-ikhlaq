## Quiz 1

**If you could watch the universe's first light (CMB) with human eyes, what color would it glow? 🌌**

A) Deep infrared (invisible)
B) Bright white like a star
C) Dull orange like a dying ember
D) Pitch black — it's long gone

Answer: C
Explanation: The CMB peaked at ~3000K when released — a warm orange glow, now redshifted to microwaves.

## Quiz 2

**Stoics say: 'We suffer more in imagination than in reality.' Which modern bias explains this best? 🧠**

A) Confirmation bias
B) Negativity bias & catastrophizing
C) Survivorship bias
D) Dunning-Kruger effect

Answer: B
Explanation: Our brains evolved to simulate worst-cases for survival — Stoicism trains us to pause that loop.
