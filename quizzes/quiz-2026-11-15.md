## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=14.2 — only 300 Myr after the Big Bang. How is it already so massive? ✨**

A) Dark matter halos collapsed earlier than we thought
B) Population III stars were far more efficient at cooling gas
C) Early black holes seeded rapid star formation via feedback
D) Our cosmological model (ΛCDM) needs new physics

Answer: A
Explanation: Simulations show massive halos can form surprisingly early in ΛCDM.

## Quiz 2

**Marcus Aurelius: 'You have power over your mind — not outside events.' What modern concept echoes this? 🧠**

A) Neuroplasticity — we rewire our responses
B) Determinism — free will is an illusion
C) Solipsism — only my mind exists
D) Nihilism — nothing matters anyway

Answer: A
Explanation: Stoic 'prohairesis' maps to our brain's ability to change its own wiring.
