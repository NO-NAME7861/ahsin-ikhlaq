## Quiz 1

**The cosmic microwave background (CMB) carries information from about how long after the universe began?**

A) 380,000 years
B) 13 billion years
C) 10−32 seconds
D) Last month

Answer: A
Explanation: The CMB is relic radiation from about 380,000 years after the Big Bang, when the universe cooled enough for electrons and nuclei to combine into neutral atoms and light could travel freely.

## Quiz 2

**Entropy is a measure of disorder. According to the second law of thermodynamics, entropy of an isolated system tends to:**

A) Increase — systems move from order to disorder
B) Decrease — life always brings more order
C) Stay constant — the universe is balanced
D) Oscillate — it rises and falls forever

Answer: A
Explanation: The second law says the entropy of an isolated system never decreases on average; that's why heat flows from hot to cold and why the arrow of time points toward disorder.
