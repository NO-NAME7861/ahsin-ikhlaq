## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy at z≈14. How old was the universe when that light began its journey? ✨**

A) ~290 million years
B) ~380 million years
C) ~520 million years
D) ~1.1 billion years

Answer: A
Explanation: z≈14 corresponds to ~290 Myr after the Big Bang — cosmic dawn.

## Quiz 2

**Marcus Aurelius wrote: 'The universe is change; our life is what our thoughts make it.' Which Stoic principle does this echo? 🌌**

A) Amor fati — love your fate
B) Dichotomy of control
C) Logos — rational order
D) Memento mori — remember death

Answer: B
Explanation: We control only our judgments; externals belong to the logos.
