## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=14.2 — what does that really tell us about the 'Dark Ages'? 🌌**

A) Reionization started earlier than models predict
B) Dark matter halos formed slower than thought
C) First stars were exclusively supermassive
D) Cosmic microwave background is contaminated

Answer: A
Explanation: High-z galaxies imply earlier UV photon production, pushing reionization onset back.

## Quiz 2

**Seneca said: 'We suffer more in imagination than in reality.' Which cognitive bias explains this best? 🧠**

A) Confirmation bias
B) Impact bias (affective forecasting error)
C) Survivorship bias
D) Dunning-Kruger effect

Answer: B
Explanation: Impact bias makes us overestimate emotional intensity and duration of future events.
