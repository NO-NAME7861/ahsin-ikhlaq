## Quiz 1

**What 'fossil light' lets us see the universe at 380,000 years old? 🌌**

A) Hubble Deep Field
B) Cosmic Microwave Background
C) Gravitational Waves
D) Quasar Spectra

Answer: B
Explanation: CMB is the cooled afterglow of the Big Bang, emitted when atoms first formed.

## Quiz 2

**The Stoics called it 'Amor Fati' — what does it truly demand? 🔥**

A) Passive acceptance of fate
B) Loving every moment as necessary
C) Predicting the future
D) Rejecting negative emotions

Answer: B
Explanation: Amor Fati means embracing all of life — including suffering — as essential to your path.
