## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy forming 300M yrs after the Big Bang — how is its light still reaching us? ✨**

A) Space expanded faster than light, stretching the photon's path
B) Light speed was faster in the early universe
C) Dark energy pulled the light toward us
D) Gravitational lensing bent it into a shortcut

Answer: A
Explanation: Cosmic expansion stretches space itself, so photons travel much farther than the simple age × c distance.

## Quiz 2

**Seneca said: 'We suffer more in imagination than in reality.' What modern bias explains this? 🧠**

A) Survivorship bias
B) Negativity bias & affective forecasting error
C) Confirmation bias
D) Dunning-Kruger effect

Answer: B
Explanation: We overestimate future emotional impact (affective forecasting) and weight threats heavily (negativity bias).
