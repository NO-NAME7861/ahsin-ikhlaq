## Quiz 1

**JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=13.2 — its light traveled 13.4 billion years. What are we actually seeing?**

A) The galaxy as it is today
B) The galaxy as it was 13.4 Gyr ago
C) A mirror image of the Milky Way
D) Dark matter halos glowing

Answer: B
Explanation: Light travel time = lookback time; we see the infant universe.

## Quiz 2

**Stoics say 'Amor Fati' — love your fate. If the universe is deterministic, what does this *really* ask of you?**

A) Passive resignation to outcomes
B) Active embrace of necessity as freedom
C) Rejection of causality entirely
D) Seeking only pleasurable fates

Answer: B
Explanation: Freedom is aligning will with logos, not fighting nature.
