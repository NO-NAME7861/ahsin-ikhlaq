## Quiz 1

**JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=14, only 290 Myr after Big Bang. How is it already so massive? 🤯**

A) Population III stars formed fast & furious
B) Dark matter halos merged earlier than models predict
C) Early supermassive black holes boosted growth
D) We're misreading redshift due to dust

Answer: A
Explanation: Metal-free Pop III stars could reach 1000 M☉, ionizing gas and driving rapid early mass assembly.

## Quiz 2

**If the universe is deterministic, does 'amor fati' (love of fate) still grant freedom? 🌌**

A) No — determinism eliminates all agency
B) Yes — freedom is aligning will with necessity
C) Only if quantum indeterminacy exists
D) Freedom requires multiverse branching

Answer: B
Explanation: Stoic freedom is internal: accepting what must happen while choosing your attitude toward it.
