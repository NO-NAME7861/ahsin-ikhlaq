## Quiz 1

**A quasar's light takes 13 billion years to reach us. When we see it, we're seeing the universe as it was... ✨**

A) 13 billion years ago
B) Right now, just far away
C) A few million years after the Big Bang
D) Before time itself began

Answer: A
Explanation: Light travel time equals lookback time; we see the past.

## Quiz 2

**The Stoics said: 'You have power over your mind, not outside events.' This is the essence of... 🧠**

A) Fatalism
B) The Dichotomy of Control
C) Hedonism
D) Determinism

Answer: B
Explanation: Epictetus: focus only on what you control — your judgments.
