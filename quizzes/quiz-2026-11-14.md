## Quiz 1

**The JWST just spotted a galaxy at z=14.2. If we could instantly teleport there today, how old would that galaxy be *now*? ✨**

A) ~290 million years
B) ~1.1 billion years
C) ~13.8 billion years
D) ~27.6 billion years

Answer: C
Explanation: Light travel time ≠ current age; the universe is ~13.8 Gyr old everywhere today.

## Quiz 2

**Marcus Aurelius: 'You have power over your mind — not outside events.' Which modern concept does this mirror best? 🧠**

A) Neuroplasticity
B) Locus of control
C) Confirmation bias
D) Cognitive dissonance

Answer: B
Explanation: Internal locus of control = Stoic focus on agency over externals.
