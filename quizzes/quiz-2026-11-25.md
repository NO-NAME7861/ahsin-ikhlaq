## Quiz 1

**Voyager 1 just crossed the heliopause. What 'bubble' did it leave behind? 🌌**

A) The Oort Cloud
B) The Heliosphere
C) The Magnetosphere
D) The Kuiper Belt

Answer: B
Explanation: The heliosphere is the Sun's protective bubble of solar wind; crossing the heliopause means entering interstellar space.

## Quiz 2

**Seneca said: 'We suffer more in imagination than in reality.' What Stoic practice fixes this? 🧠**

A) Amor Fati (Love of Fate)
B) Premeditatio Malorum
C) Memento Mori
D) Dichotomy of Control

Answer: B
Explanation: Premeditatio Malorum (premeditation of evils) trains the mind to face fears beforehand, robbing anxiety of its power.
