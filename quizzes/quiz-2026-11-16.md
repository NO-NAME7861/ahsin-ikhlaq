## Quiz 1

**If you fell into a black hole, what would the universe look like just before the singularity? ✨**

A) A blinding white flash of all future light
B) A tiny, blueshifted dot of the entire cosmic history
C) Total darkness — light cannot catch you
D) A frozen, redshifted snapshot of the moment you crossed the horizon

Answer: B
Explanation: Extreme blueshift compresses all incoming light into a bright, shrinking dot overhead.

## Quiz 2

**The Stoics called it 'Amor Fati' — love of fate. What does it actually demand of us? 🌿**

A) Passive resignation to whatever happens
B) Active embrace of every event as necessary fuel for virtue
C) Seeking pleasure to balance life's inevitable pains
D) Detaching from outcomes to avoid suffering

Answer: B
Explanation: Amor Fati means willing what happens, not just enduring it — turning obstacles into practice.
