## Quiz 1

**Voyager 1 just crossed the heliopause. What 'bubble' did it leave behind? 🌌**

A) Oort Cloud
B) Heliosphere
C) Magnetosphere
D) Kuiper Belt

Answer: B
Explanation: The heliosphere is the Sun's protective bubble of solar wind.

## Quiz 2

**Marcus Aurelius: 'You have power over your mind — not outside events.' This is core to which philosophy?**

A) Epicureanism
B) Cynicism
C) Stoicism
D) Existentialism

Answer: C
Explanation: Stoicism teaches focusing only on what you control: your judgments.
