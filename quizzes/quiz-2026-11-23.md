## Quiz 1

**JWST just spotted a galaxy at z ≈ 13.2 — only 320 Myr after the Big Bang. How did it grow so massive so fast? ✨**

A) Primordial black hole seeds
B) Cold gas streams feeding super-Eddington bursts
C) Modified gravity (MOND) at high-z
D) Population III supernova enrichment

Answer: B
Explanation: Cold filaments deliver gas faster than feedback can stop it, enabling rapid stellar mass buildup.

## Quiz 2

**The Stoics called it *amor fati* — love of fate. What does it actually demand of us? 🧭**

A) Blind acceptance of all outcomes
B) Active embrace: treat every event as necessary fuel for virtue
C) Suppressing emotion to achieve apathy
D) Predicting the future through reason alone

Answer: B
Explanation: Amor fati isn't passive resignation; it's saying 'this is what I needed' and acting accordingly.
