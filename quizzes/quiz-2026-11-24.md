## Quiz 1

**Light from the earliest galaxies is redshifted into infrared. Why is JWST essential for seeing them?**

A) Hubble cannot detect infrared at all
B) Earth's atmosphere blocks those wavelengths
C) Infrared penetrates cosmic dust better
D) Only JWST has a large enough mirror

Answer: B
Explanation: Atmospheric absorption makes ground IR astronomy impossible; space telescopes are mandatory.

## Quiz 2

**The Stoics said we suffer more in imagination than reality. Which cognitive bias mirrors this?**

A) Confirmation bias
B) Negativity bias
C) Impact bias
D) Survivorship bias

Answer: C
Explanation: Impact bias: we overestimate emotional intensity and duration of future events.
