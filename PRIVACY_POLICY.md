# Privacy Policy — Ahsin Akhlaq YouTube Automation (Universe & Philosophy)

**Last updated: November 2026**

This policy covers the "UniverseExplorer" application (a personal, unverified automation tool) operated by the owner of the YouTube channel associated with **Ahsin Akhlaq** (handle to be confirmed).

## What this app does
The app uploads videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and, optionally, backs them up to the operator's Google Drive. It uses Google APIs as an uploader of the operator's own content.

## Data we collect
- **None.** The app is run by and for the channel owner. It does not collect, sell, or share any user data. It does not track other people's accounts.

## Data processed
- OAuth access/refresh tokens are used solely to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Drive. They are stored as GitHub Actions secrets (encrypted) and in a local file (`token.json`) that is never shared or committed to git.
- Script prompts and generated content (videos/images) are processed via third-party services (GitHub Actions, OpenRouter, Pollinations.ai, Microsoft Edge TTS) to produce the owner's own content. Those processors operate under their own privacy policies.

## Data sharing
No data is shared with any third party other than the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app. No ads, no analytics trackers.

## Children's privacy
This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Changes
This policy may be updated over time. Changes are reflected in the commit history of this repository.
